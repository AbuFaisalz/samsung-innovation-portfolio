The future we prepare for.

Three forces reshape what customers value. Compatibility becomes the expectation; outcomes become the advantage.

ENERGY

The home becomes an energy partner.

Tighter grids and efficiency standards push appliances to coordinate consumption around prices, solar generation and household needs.

A day in 2036.

Across Asia’s cities, air conditioners cool rooms before peak tariffs, washing machines wait for cheaper power, and appliances from competing brands coordinate through open standards.

In a Bangkok apartment, Mei watches short videos on her phone while her home quietly manages its energy budget. The television stays dark until friends arrive for a football match.

When she buys an appliance, she compares lifetime running costs, repairability and compatibility—not whether every device carries the same logo.

Adapted from the board: a possible future, not a forecast.
